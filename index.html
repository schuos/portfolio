Shi En's Portfolio

Creative Statement

Hi, my name is Shi En! I am a student at Hunter College, and taking a course in Visual Communications and Interactive Design mostly for fun. As someone who does not have much experience in using the different softwares by Adobe, my works coming out of this class are mostly experimental, as I play with the different tools it provides. Many of the photos I take and use for my works have a narrative or experience I want to depict. Because I do not have much experience, I am excited to learn about the different ways I can apply these new skills into my own hobbies, for example, when I draw outside of class, or edit photos that I take. Covered in my works are my own observations of the world around me. That could be what I see around Central Park, such as trends, or an unusually empty library on my college campus, an uncanny memory. Throughout the class, I learned different elements in creating media such as in photography where I learned about the manipulation of depth of field and shutter speed, both influencing the amount of blur in a photo. I used Photoshop and Illustrator to edit and make these images, where I learned, for example, how to adjust the lighting and saturation of an image to better focus on a subject. By the end of this class, I hope to be able to navigate better in Photoshop compared to the beginning, and recognize the use of visual elements in the world around me and how it influences me.
